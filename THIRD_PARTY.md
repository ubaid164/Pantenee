# Third-party components

- OpenAI Whisper and the SYSTRAN CTranslate2 conversion use the MIT license. The [Whisper license](licenses/Whisper-MIT.txt) is included. [Pinned Whisper Small conversion](https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-small/tree/536b0662742c02347bc0e980a01041f333bce120)
- Faster Whisper uses the MIT license. [Faster Whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper)
- Silero VAD uses the MIT license. [Silero VAD](https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad)
- Optional text translation uses the Apache-2.0 Qwen3.5-2B model and the MIT-licensed llama.cpp and llama-cpp-python runtime. [Qwen3.5 weights](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B) · [Quantized download](https://ollama.com/library/qwen3.5:2b) · [llama-cpp-python](https://github.com/abetlen/llama-cpp-python)
- Other runtime components include CTranslate2, ONNX Runtime, NumPy, PyAV and FFmpeg, sounddevice and PortAudio, and Python/Tcl/Tk. The release includes dependency notices where provided by the installed distributions. [CTranslate2](https://github.com/OpenNMT/CTranslate2) · [ONNX Runtime](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime) · [PyAV](https://github.com/PyAV-Org/PyAV) · [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/) · [PortAudio](https://github.com/PortAudio/portaudio)
